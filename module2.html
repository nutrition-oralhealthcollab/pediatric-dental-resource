Oral Disease Recognition for Non-Dental Providers

Essential clinical indicators for the early detection and management of common pediatric oral pathologies by primary care teams.

verified Standards Alignment

Aligned with AAPD Reference Manual & ABPD 2024 Clinical Standards.

warning

emergency Early Childhood Caries (ECC)

Early detection by non-dental providers is the frontline defense. Identify "white spot" lesions along the gingival margin before cavitation occurs.

info Stage 1: Incipient

Chalky white opacities; demineralization of the enamel surface.

error Stage 2: Cavitated

Brown/Yellow lesions with structural loss. Immediate referral required.
