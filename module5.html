Special Populations & Interprofessional Care

Managing complex interactions between gastrointestinal health, homeostatic balance in G-tube patients, and long-term dental integrity.

health_metrics GERD & Dental Erosion Management

Pathological reflux introduces gastric acid (pH < 2.0) into the oral cavity, leading to irreversible mineral loss. Management requires a synergistic approach between gastroenterology and pediatric dentistry.

chemistry Chemical Buffering

Use of sodium bicarbonate rinses post-emesis episodes to neutralize pH.

verified_user Protective Barriers

High-viscosity glass ionomer sealants for exposed dentin and enamel protection.

Interprofessional Action

  • Medication Review Verify PPI medication timing (Omeprazole) for peak efficacy.
  • Nocturnal Triggers Assess sleeping position and late-night feeding reflux triggers.
  • Recalls Dental exams every 3 months for active erosion cases.
  • Oral Care Avoid immediate brushing after reflux; rinse first.
